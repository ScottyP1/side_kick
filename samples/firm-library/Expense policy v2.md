# Fabrikam Advisory – Expense policy (version 2)

Effective 1 January 2025. Replaced by version 3 on 1 July 2026.

- Meals while travelling for a client: up to $60 per person per day, including tips.
- Receipts are required for every expense over $40.
- Client entertainment needs the engagement manager's approval before the event.
- Submit expenses within 45 days.
