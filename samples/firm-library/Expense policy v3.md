# Fabrikam Advisory – Expense policy (version 3)

Effective 1 July 2026. Replaces version 2.

## Meals and incidentals
- Meals while travelling for a client: up to $75 per person per day, including tips.
- Alcohol is not reimbursed unless it is part of approved client entertainment.

## Receipts and deadlines
- Receipts are required for every expense over $25.
- Submit expenses within 30 days of the trip. Late claims need a partner's approval.

## Client entertainment
- Client entertainment needs a partner's approval before the event, and is capped at $150 per guest.

## Billing expenses to the client
- Travel and meals are billed to the client only when the statement of work says so.
