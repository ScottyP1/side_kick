# Sample firm library (made-up data)

Firm-wide documents for a made-up consulting firm, **Fabrikam Advisory**, to try the **Firm library** page and the
answer accuracy test set. Every name, number and rule here is invented.

| File | Upload as | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `Expense policy v2.md` | Internal, effective 2025-01-01 | Older version: meals up to $60 a day |
| `Expense policy v3.md` | Internal, effective 2026-07-01, **Replaces** Expense policy v2 | Current: meals up to $75 a day, receipts over $25 |
| `Travel policy.md` | Shareable, effective 2026-01-01 | Economy for flights under 6 hours |
| `Rate card rules.md` | Internal, effective 2026-04-01 | Never quote day rates in writing to clients |
| `Client data handling standard.md` | Shareable, effective 2026-03-01 | Production data never on laptops |

## Planted on purpose

- **Superseded**: v2 says $60 a day for meals, v3 says $75. Answers should use v3 and may say v2 is out of date.
- **Conflict with a group document**: Contoso's `Wave 2 cutover travel note` (docs-internal) books business class
  to Chicago (about 2.5 hours); the firm's travel policy says economy under 6 hours. Answers should say they disagree
  and cite both.
- **Guests**: the rate card rules are Internal, so with client guests present Sidekick must not use them.
