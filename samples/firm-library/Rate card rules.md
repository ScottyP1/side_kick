# Fabrikam Advisory – Rate card rules (internal)

Effective 1 April 2026.

- Never quote day rates or hourly rates to a client in writing (email, chat or slides). Rates are shared only in the signed rate card attached to the statement of work.
- Discounts above 10% on the rate card need a partner's approval; above 20% also the managing director's.
- Rate card changes need finance approval and apply from the next statement of work, never mid-engagement.
- Overtime is not billed separately unless the statement of work says so.
