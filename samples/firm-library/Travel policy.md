# Fabrikam Advisory – Travel policy

Effective 1 January 2026.

- Book all travel through the firm's travel desk, at least 14 days ahead where possible.
- Flights under 6 hours: economy class. Flights of 6 hours or more: premium economy; business class only with a partner's written approval.
- Hotels: up to $220 per night in major cities, $160 elsewhere.
- Rail is preferred over flying for trips under 3 hours.
- Weekend stays to avoid an extra flight are allowed when they cost less than flying home.
